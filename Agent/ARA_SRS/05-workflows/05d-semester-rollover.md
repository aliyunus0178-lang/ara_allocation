# 5d. Semester Rollover
**Doc ID:** SRS-ARA-05d | **Parent:** [README.md](../README.md)

At the start of a new academic year/semester, the ARA Administrator
shall be able to configure, per responsibility type (block, room,
course):

- **Auto-carry-forward** — the prior semester's responsibility
  assignment is copied forward as a new record (new start/end dates)
  pending administrator confirmation.
- **Manual re-assignment required** — all responsibilities expire at
  semester end and must be explicitly re-assigned.

Default behavior is configurable at the institution level, and
overridable per block/room/course.
